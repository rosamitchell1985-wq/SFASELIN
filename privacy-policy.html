Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Applies to sfaselin.shop

Sfaselin ("we", "us", "our") operates sfaselin.shop. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using our website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Order details: name, shipping address, phone number and email address, used to fulfil and deliver your order.
  • Payment choice: payment is processed by PCI-compliant payment partners; we never see or store your full card number, CVV or PayPal credentials.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form, email or text.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you join the Glow List.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, pages visited, and general location (city-level) — used to keep the site fast and relevant.
  • Your bag and wishlist contents are stored in your own browser (local storage) and are not transmitted to us until you place an order.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack, ship and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery notifications by email.
  • To respond to your questions and handle returns, exchanges and anti-tarnish claims.
  • To send marketing emails only if you subscribe — every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our store secure.

3. Who We Share It With

We share the minimum necessary, only with:

  • Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx — name and address) to deliver your package.
  • Payment processors to complete transactions.
  • Law enforcement or regulators where disclosure is legally required.

We never sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your bag, wishlist and coupon between visits, and essential cookies to keep the site working. You can clear these any time from your browser settings; the site will still work, but your bag will reset.

5. Data Retention & Security

Order records are retained as required under US tax law (typically seven years). Contact messages are kept for 24 months. We use HTTPS encryption in transit and restrict access to personal data to staff who need it to serve you.

6. Your Rights (Including CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your state (including California, Virginia, Colorado and others), you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of it (subject to legal retention duties).
  • Opt out of marketing at any time.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these, email support@sfaselin.shop with the subject "Privacy Request". We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Our store is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, announce it on the website before it takes effect.
