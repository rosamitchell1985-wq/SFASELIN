Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Applies to sfaselin.shop

1. Where We Ship

We currently ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship internationally yet — join the Glow List on our home page to hear when we do.

2. Processing Time

  • Orders placed before 2pm ET are packed at our New York studio the same or next business day.
  • All orders ship within 1–2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or federal holidays ship the next business day.

3. Delivery Timelines

  • Contiguous US: 2–5 business days via USPS or UPS.
  • Alaska, Hawaii & US territories: 5–10 business days.

These are estimates, not guarantees — carrier delays during peak season or severe weather can add a day or two.

4. Shipping Charges

  • Free standard shipping on all orders of $35 and above (after coupons).
  • A flat $4.99 shipping fee applies to orders under $35.

5. Tracking Your Order

The moment your order ships, you receive a tracking link by email. You can follow your package door to door. If tracking hasn't updated in 5 business days, contact us and we'll chase the carrier for you.

6. Packaging

Every piece travels in a padded Sfaselin pouch inside a rigid, tamper-evident box — gift-ready at no extra charge. Sending a gift? Message us with your order ID and we'll handwrite a card.

7. Delivery Issues

  • Failed deliveries: if a package returns to us after failed attempts, we'll re-ship once free of charge.
  • Lost packages: if tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, we resend or refund in full — your choice.
  • Damaged in transit: see our Returns & Refunds policy — photo within 48 hours and we replace it free.
  • Marked delivered but not received: check with neighbors and your carrier first, then contact us within 7 days and we'll open a carrier investigation and make it right.
