Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Applies to sfaselin.shop

We want you to love every piece. If something isn't right, here's exactly how we make it right — no fine-print surprises.

1. The 30-Day Return Window

  • You may return any unworn piece within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.
  • The piece must be in its original condition: unworn, with tags, pouch and box intact.
  • For hygiene reasons, earrings that have been worn cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

2. Damaged, Defective or Wrong Item

  • Email support@sfaselin.shop within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and a clear photo of the issue.
  • We email you a prepaid return label and send a replacement immediately, or refund you in full — your choice.
  • No questions asked, no return shipping cost to you.

3. How to Start a Return

  • Email support@sfaselin.shop or text +1 (212) 555-0147 with your order ID (starts with SF) and the pieces you wish to return.
  • We confirm eligibility within one business day and email you a prepaid USPS return label.
  • Pack the piece securely in its original box, attach the label, and drop it at any USPS location.

4. Refund Timelines

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method (card, PayPal or Apple Pay) within 5–7 business days after the returned piece arrives and passes inspection.
  • Shipping fees (if any) are refunded only when the return is due to our error.
  • Coupon discounts are not re-issued; if a coupon was applied, the refunded amount reflects the discounted price paid.
  • Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 2–3 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

5. Exchanges

Prefer a different piece or size? Mark your return as an exchange and we'll ship the new piece as soon as your return label shows first movement — you pay or are refunded only the difference.

6. Anti-Tarnish Claims

Tarnishing within 6 months under normal wear is covered by our anti-tarnish promise and is handled as a free one-time exchange — see our Terms & Conditions, clause 5.

7. What Isn't Returnable

  • Pieces showing signs of wear, perfume damage or missing components.
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.
  • Gift pouches or packaging returned without the product.

8. Questions

Write to support@sfaselin.shop or call +1 (212) 555-0147 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET). We reply within one business day.
