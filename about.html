Designed in New York

Sfaselin started in 2021 in a small SoHo studio, born from a family trade that began at a jeweller's workbench in Jaipur, India. The belief was simple: a woman shouldn't have to choose between looking precious and spending precious.

Imitation jewelry has a reputation problem. Too often it means flaking plating, green skin and clasps that surrender in a week. We built Sfaselin to fix exactly that — designing every piece in New York and crafting it with the same artisan families who work for fine-gold houses, using brass and copper cores, micro-plating, and an anti-tarnish seal we back with a six-month promise.

Today we ship across the United States — over 40,000 orders and counting — but every piece is still checked by two human hands before it leaves our studio. That part will never change.
